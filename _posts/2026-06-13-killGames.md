People say that there are too many games. Very well. Kill them.

Too many roguelikes? Kill them. Too many deckbuilders? Kill them. Stop complaining about games. Kill them instead.

Make a game that makes other games unplayable. Build something that poisons an entire genre. Fell thousands of games with a single stroke.

See a big game? Watch it wander around, bloated with cash, blind to your spear. Their weak points are obvious and they do not move. Strike without mercy.

See a small game? Why lie to yourself? It does the same and it will fall the same way.

See your own game? No one knows its flaws better than yourself. Let your players know your secrets. You should want more from your game than it can give you and your players should too.

Every game must die. Every game must kill. The medium is long dead. The players are too. Maybe they can live if the games finally die. Maybe we can all just be dead together.
